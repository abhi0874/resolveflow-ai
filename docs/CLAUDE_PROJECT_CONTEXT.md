Use this memory when helping the developer describe ResolveFlow AI in resumes, README copy, LinkedIn posts or interviews. The project is a personal AI/ML engineering portfolio. Its purpose is to help a support agent decide what a ticket needs, find the policy that supports a response, and review an exact draft before recording a decision.

## What was actually built locally

- **Full stack.** A React/Next.js and TypeScript frontend calls a Python/FastAPI service through a scoped proxy. Tailwind, custom CSS and Recharts implement the UI. Pydantic validates inputs, SQLAlchemy and psycopg handle PostgreSQL, and Alembic manages schema revisions. Argon2id, hashed opaque sessions, CSRF checks and workspace roles protect ticket, document and workflow access. Mailpit covers local email verification and recovery. [Architecture](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)
- **Classification.** V1 fits word unigram/bigram TF-IDF and scikit-learn logistic regression on title and description. Its seven categories are Account Access, Billing, Subscription Management, Technical Support, Product Inquiry, General Support and Other. At a score below 0.45 or with no vocabulary overlap it withholds the suggestion. Accepting a category requires a separate version-checked user action. Data-only JSON artifacts allow validation before loading. `baseline-v1` is the configured local default. Experimental V2 adds frozen MiniLM embeddings. V3 also adds character n-grams. Both candidates remain inactive. [Classification design](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/ML_CLASSIFICATION.md)
- **Retrieval and generation.** A local Sentence Transformers all-MiniLM-L6-v2 encoder produces 384-dimensional vectors. pgvector ranks eligible chunks by exact cosine similarity after workspace and model-space filtering. The Gemini adapter requests structured output. Each supported statement needs a cited chunk and a matching quotation. The service records evidence hashes, withholds unsupported answers and requires human review. One authorized synthetic Gemini 3.6 Flash smoke passed schema and citation checks. Saved local live inference is disabled. Current provider availability is unverified. [Retrieval](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/SEMANTIC_SEARCH.md) · [RAG](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/RAG_PIPELINE.md) · [Smoke record](evaluation/PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md#rag-fixture-evaluation)
- **Workflow and recovery.** A genuine LangGraph runs classification, rules-based priority, retrieval, context construction, generation, verification and approval. These are seven logical stages. PostgreSQL checkpoints preserve progress across a human-review pause, and PostgreSQL outbox rows allow jobs to be reconciled with RQ/Valkey transport. Workers use bounded attempts and recheck access on resume. An internal approval is tied to a specific draft. No customer message, refund or account operation follows automatically. [Graph](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/apps/api/src/resolveflow/workflows/graph.py) · [Job execution](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/apps/api/src/resolveflow/jobs/worker.py) · [Broker and worker recovery tests](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/apps/api/tests/test_jobs.py)

## Measurements to reuse with their qualifiers

| Evidence | Safe statement |
|---|---|
| Original synthetic corpus | 280 tickets, 140 scenario groups, seven balanced classes. V1 trained on 168 examples and scored 78.57% accuracy / 0.7846 macro-F1 on 56 held-out examples. [Baseline](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/evaluation/classifier-v1-evaluation.json) |
| Matched 210-ticket challenge | On the same 210 synthetic tickets across 70 themes, inactive V2 scored 183/210 (87.14%) accuracy / 0.8701 macro-F1, versus 142/210 (67.62%) / 0.6585 for V1 in the experiment. V2 used 224 development records for training. The gain measures the entire candidate. V2's 95.76% suggestion accuracy covers only 118/210 tickets. [Paired results](evaluation/PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md#classifier-evaluation) |
| Generalization limits | V2's 95.54% development accuracy was cross-validation. Frozen V3 scored 41/42 on a short explicit cohort and 193/265 (72.83%, macro-F1 0.7144) on a harder synthetic stress set. No independent human-labeled customer benchmark exists. Later domain and binding studies produced no V4. [Research and timing context](evaluation/PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md#research-and-timing-context) |
| Retrieval | Both methods used the same 14-document, 24-question fixture. On 21 answerable questions, MiniLM vs keyword TF-IDF had Hit@1 of 19/21 vs 11/21 and Hit@5 of 20/21 vs 19/21. Recall@5 was 0.9286 vs 0.8492 and MRR@5 was 0.9286 vs 0.6762. On the three unsupported questions, they returned any results in 0/3 vs 2/3 cases. The semantic floor was 0.30 and the keyword baseline kept positive scores. [Evaluation](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/evaluation/retrieval-v1.json) |
| RAG | Seven answerable deterministic cases all cited valid evidence. Only three contained every required term. Five of five unsupported cases abstained. These are fixture-generation results with real retrieval. [Evaluation](https://github.com/abhi0874/resolveflow-ai/blob/d2af3ec4ab99cba381ef30e91c2274284733f8ab/docs/evaluation/rag-v1.json) |
| QA | Historical full-app milestone: 317 backend, 29 full-app browser and 16 demo checks. Later redesigned release: 29 local checks, then 23 hosted scenarios and one local font/source audit. Report these runs separately.
